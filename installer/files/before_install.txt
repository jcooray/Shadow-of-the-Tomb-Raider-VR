Before you install
------------------

- Close Shadow of the Tomb Raider (and any mod tools running with it).
- The mod is installed into the game's folder: Setup finds it in your Steam, Epic or GOG library. Nothing in the
  game's own files is changed; uninstall removes the mod again.
- If another mod uses dxgi.dll in the game folder (ReShade, OptiScaler, Special K), Setup moves it aside and puts it
  back when you uninstall. The two can't run together yet.
- Optional: foveated rendering (CheekyFoveatedDLSS), fixed or eye tracked, and with eye tracking DLSS 5 neural
  rendering. The next pages explain each.
