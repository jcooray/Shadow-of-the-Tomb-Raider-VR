CheekyFoveatedDLSS (optional part of Shadow of the Tomb Raider VR)
==================================================================

The files in the CheekyFoveatedDLSS folder are CheekyFoveatedDLSS by ClarkCheekyKent and contributors (the VR mod loads
them; Cheeky's own version.dll loader isn't installed),
built from upstream v0.6.0 with three changes offered upstream (ClarkCheekyKent/CheekyFoveatedDLSS pull requests
#64-#66), including the interface that lets the VR mod supply the gaze and each eye's view, and a Logging setting
(installed off; the VR mod's overlay turns it on for bug reports).
